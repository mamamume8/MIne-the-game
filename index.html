Present Simple Practice

🎭 Mime the Action!

Act out everyday activities without speaking, while your classmates guess what you are doing!

🎲 How to Play

  • 1
    The Actor: Click the big button below to get a secret daily routine. Don't show anyone!
  • 2
    Action: Act out the routine using only your body. No words, no sounds!
  • 3
    The Class: Watch the actor and shout out correct English sentences to win a point!

🧠 Grammar Rules

To guess correctly, you must use complete sentences!

✅ Affirmative (He/She)
"He brushes his teeth!"
❓ Question
"Do you wake up early?"
❌ Negative (Guessing Wrong)
"No, he doesn't read a book."
